LockDesk Support

LockDesk is the dispatch app for the staff of Legend Locksmith Inc. Here's how to get help.

Contact

Email support@lockdeskapp.com and include what you were doing and what you saw. We usually reply within one business day.

Common questions

I made an account but can't see anything.
New accounts wait for the owner's approval. Once approved, tap Check again.

I forgot my password.
On the sign in screen tap Forgot password. The owner will set a temporary password and send it to you.

I don't see a job I was told about.
Technicians only see jobs assigned to them. Ask the office to assign it to you.

How do I delete my account?
Open Account in the app and tap Delete my account.
