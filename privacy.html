Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

LockDesk is a dispatch and job management app used by the staff of Legend Locksmith Inc. It is developed by Zevi Neiman. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

Who can use LockDesk

Only staff accounts approved by the business owner can sign in. New accounts see nothing until they are approved.

Information we collect

  • Staff accounts: name, email address, phone number (optional), role, and sign in activity.
  • Customer and job records entered by staff: customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, service addresses, vehicle and lock details, job notes, prices, invoices, and payment records.
  • Payments: only the amount, method, date and a reference number. LockDesk never collects or stores card numbers or bank details. Cards are processed separately through the business's payment processor.

How information is used

Information is used only to run the business's dispatch operation: scheduling and assigning jobs, showing technicians the jobs assigned to them, creating invoices and receipts, and keeping job history. It is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. LockDesk has no ads and no third party tracking or analytics.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in data centers in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is enforced by the database itself: technicians can only see jobs assigned to them, and only the owner can approve accounts.

Service providers

Supabase (database and sign in) and GitHub Pages (hosting the app). If invoice emails are enabled, Resend delivers them. These providers process data only to provide their service.

Keeping and deleting data

Job and invoice records are kept as business records for as long as the business needs them. Any staff member can delete their own account from Account inside the app. The owner can remove accounts, and delete customers and jobs. To ask about or request deletion of your information, contact us below.

Children

LockDesk is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
